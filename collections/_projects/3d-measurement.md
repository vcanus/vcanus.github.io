## Measurement Devices — The Right 3D Sensor for Every Purpose

VCANUS selects and combines two measurement modalities according to the target, precision, and environment. Zeiss ATOS5 is one of these devices, applied when optical full-field scanning is required.

### Optical Full-Field Scanner — Zeiss ATOS5 Series

Using structured (blue) light, it captures high-density full-field data of up to 12 million points per scan, contactless. It rapidly digitizes the entire surface of complex geometries, and the model can be selected by measurement volume: ATOS 5 / ATOS 5 for Airfoil / ATOS 5X / ATOS LRX.

<img src="/assets/images/gen/projects/roboscan-atos5.webp" alt="Zeiss ATOS5 series — spec comparison of ATOS LRX, ATOS 5 for Airfoil, ATOS 5, ATOS 5X" style="display:block; width:100%; height:auto; max-width:1100px; margin:1.5rem auto;">

Key advantages:
- High-resolution 3D data for detailed part analysis
- Blue light and laser technology for stable measurements in industrial environments
- Fast data acquisition (millions of points per second)
- Compatibility with ZEISS INSPECT, PolyWorks, and VCANUS analytics software

### 2D/3D Profile Displacement Sensor — Inline Shape & Height Measurement

Using the laser light-section method, it measures the surface height (Z) profile and scans the line across to build a full-field 3D height map. Since it discriminates height differences regardless of color or pattern, it reliably detects fine unevenness and dents even within the same color family, making it well suited for high-speed inline inspection.

<img src="/assets/images/gen/projects/profile-sensor-concept.svg" alt="2D/3D profile displacement sensor principle — laser line profile and 3D height map" style="display:block; width:100%; height:auto; max-width:1000px; margin:1.5rem auto;">

Key advantages:
- Quantitative inspection of height, depth, step, and volume
- Stable 3D detection unaffected by color, pattern, or focus
- High-speed inline measurement with no software development required
- Full-field 3D inspection of large targets with wide-field heads

### 2. AI & Statistical Analysis Software

VCANUS provides advanced software tools to transform raw 3D data into actionable insights:

1) Tolerance-Based Classification:
- Apply production-specific tolerances to automatically classify parts as within/outside control limits.
- Generate pass/fail reports based on positional tolerances (e.g., GD&T).
2) Statistical Process Control (SPC):
- Track dimensional variations over time to detect trends, shifts, or drifts.
- Correlate measurement data with process parameters (e.g., temperature, pressure) to identify root causes.
3) AI-Powered Anomaly Detection:
- Use machine learning models to detect unexpected patterns in measurement data.
- Predict potential issues before they affect production quality.
> Example Workflow (Automotive Mass Production):
>   - Scan: Robot-mounted ATOS captures 3D data of a car body panel.
>   - Align: Software aligns scan data to CAD nominals.
>   - Analyze:
>       - Compare measurements against management tolerances (e.g., ±0.2 mm for critical features).
>       - Apply statistical models to assess process stability.
>   - Report:
>       - Automated reports highlight areas exceeding tolerances.
>       - Dashboards visualize trends and deviations for process engineers.
>   - Act:
>       - Adjust production parameters (e.g., fixture settings, machining paths) based on data.
