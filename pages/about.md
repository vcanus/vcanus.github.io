VCANUS specializes in real-time data processing, AI-driven analytics, and adaptive control systems, helping industries tackle complex challenges with data-driven solutions. Our expertise in transforming raw data into actionable insights enables smarter decision-making, advanced automation, and optimization—whether in manufacturing, education, healthcare, or beyond.


## Core Capabilities

### 1. High-Speed, Low-Latency Data Processing & AI Models
- **High-speed, low-latency data processing**: From fieldbus and data-infrastructure connectivity to real-time (RT) data processing and high-performance visualization — combined with edge and MLOps to ingest and process thousands of sensor/signal inputs per second at low latency. (Powered by the TSLoom time-series platform)
- **AI-based analytics models**: Statistical and machine-learning models for forecasting, anomaly detection, and root-cause analysis, operable even by non-experts through visual workflows.
- **Image analysis**: Combining FPGA-based high-speed image processing (frame grabbers, Gidel) with platform-based image processing (the DeepVi AI vision platform) — covering edge detection and distance/displacement through dataset and labeling, inline processing, model training and inference, and review.

### 2. 3D Measurement & Inspection
- **3D shape measurement**: High-density 3D digitization with ZEISS ATOS optical scanners and 3D profile sensors.
- **3D displacement & strain measurement**: Full-field 3D displacement and strain measurement with ZEISS ARAMIS.
- **In-situ shape inspection**: Robot/AMR-based measurement directly on the production floor, using data stitching and registration to inspect the full shape of large objects. (RoboScan)
- **Digital twin**: Pre-validation and simulation of measurement sequences in a virtual environment.

### 3. Intelligent Control & Patterning Systems
- **High-speed synchronized control**: Multi-axis high-speed synchronized control, including 3D OTF (On-The-Fly) control, for real-time precision tasks.
- **Intelligent path generation**: Automatic optimal-path generation for robots, lasers, and automated systems, validated with digital twins. (VRCAM)
- **Laser patterning systems**: 2D/3D patterning and material removal / circuit patterning for FPD and semiconductor manufacturing and machining processes.
- **Adaptive systems**: Real-time adaptive control and hybrid PID + AI self-optimization for stable, efficient operation. (VURT-X)
